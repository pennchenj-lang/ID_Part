# Paper experiment protocols

The [current data index](../paper_data/DATA_INDEX.md) maps each reported experiment to its source tables and frozen protocol. The fine-ID evaluation, later Group regression, original holdout, full-image discovery, learned-method comparison and routing evaluation have distinct prediction pools or endpoints; use the protocol for the corresponding study.

The 65-case cohort is development data. The 226-case Group analyses are regression analyses on reused images. The original 42-object Group holdout and its subsequent full-image/crop comparisons are identified separately. The expanded routing study contains 37 historical and 216 additional requests.
